### Hi, I'm Jordan 👋

I'm a **VFX artist who builds tools by directing AI coding agents**.

I come from production VFX, where the bar is set by eye. I bring that bar to software: I define what
"right" looks like, build the tests that prove it, and steer AI agents (Claude Code, Codex) through
large, hard projects until the result holds up to that standard.

**Projects**

🎬 **[A real-time render engine inside Maya's viewport](https://github.com/jlipstock/maya-cloudscape)**:
Nanite-style streamed geometry with dynamic decimation, art-directable volumetric clouds and god rays,
RTX ray-traced shadows, and a film-quality final render matched against Arnold. ([videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3wv_kF-BnFU))

🌌 **[Starscape](https://github.com/jlipstock/starscape)**: a 360° deep-space sky generator for VFX. It builds stars,
a photo-driven Milky Way, nebulae and galaxies, exports 16K HDR environments, and turns a Hubble photo into a 3D particle galaxy.

🏰 **[Procedural castle builder](https://github.com/jlipstock/procedural-castle-builder)**: a cozy Roblox building game in
the spirit of Tiny Glade. Draw a line and a wall appears, with doors, windows, roofs, stairs, paths and flowers filling
themselves in. Multiplayer-ready, ~680 headless tests.

🟢 **[Slime World](https://github.com/jlipstock/slime-world)**: a Roblox game with film-quality environments I sculpted and
textured myself, plus custom water, caustics, fish schools and bird flocks, holding 60 fps on phones.

🎮 **[Roblox Studio tools](https://github.com/jlipstock/roblox-studio-tools)**: 22 plugins (~70k lines of Luau) bringing a VFX
toolset to Roblox: real-time water, fish schools and bird flocks, scatter, fog, caustics and a Maya/Houdini bridge,
built for a live game that holds 60 fps on phones.

📈 **[Whatnot Stream Tracker](https://github.com/jlipstock/whatnot-stream-tracker)**: a Chrome extension for a live-selling business.
It quietly scans the platform every 30 minutes and turns the data into a weekly heatmap of the best low-competition
times to go live, with an AI advice tab on top.

🧠 **[Trading research brain](https://github.com/jlipstock/trading-research-brain)**: an AI research lab that tries to prove
its own trading ideas wrong. Agents propose, debate, test and learn under honesty rules enforced in code (pre-declared bars,
a luck ledger, look-ahead guards), plus the trading system that routes orders to Interactive Brokers behind a fidelity gate.
